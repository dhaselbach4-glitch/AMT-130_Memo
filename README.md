2. **Probability of success:** anchored on the 86.7% BLA-to-approval rate for neurology (BIO / Informa / QLS 2021), with each adjustment stated and sourced.
3. **rNPV:** patients × price, capped each year by neurosurgical capacity and in total by the eligible pool.
4. **What would make me wrong:** every downside case, priced.
5. **What would change my mind:** the disclosures and dates to watch.
